# 📱 COVID-19 Vaccine Tracker - Mobile App Installation Guide

Welcome! This guide will help you install the COVID-19 Vaccine Tracker as a mobile app on your device.

## What is a Progressive Web App (PWA)?

A Progressive Web App allows you to install this website as a mobile application on your device. Once installed, it will:

- ✅ Appear on your home screen with an app icon
- ✅ Launch in full-screen mode (no browser UI)
- ✅ Work faster with offline caching
- ✅ Provide a native app-like experience

---

## 📱 Installation Instructions

### Android (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox)

#### **Method 1: Using the Install Banner**

1. Open the COVID-19 Vaccine Tracker in Chrome/Edge
2. A banner will appear at the bottom saying "Add VaxTracker to Home screen"
3. Tap **"Install"** or **"Add"**
4. The app will be installed and appear on your home screen

#### **Method 2: Using the Browser Menu**

1. Open the app in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox
2. Tap the **menu icon** (⋮) in the top-right corner
3. Select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**
4. Enter a name (default: "VaxTracker") and tap **"Add"**
5. The app icon will appear on your home screen

#### **Launching the App**

- Find the "VaxTracker" icon on your home screen
- Tap it to launch the app in standalone mode
- Enjoy a full-screen, native-like experience!

---

### iPhone/iPad (iOS - Safari Only)

> **⚠️ Important:** On iOS, PWA installation ONLY works with Safari. Chrome and other browsers on iOS don't support PWA installation.

#### **Step-by-Step Instructions**

1. **Open Safari** and navigate to the COVID-19 Vaccine Tracker

2. **Tap the Share Button** (□↑)
   - Located at the bottom of the screen on iPhone
   - Located at the top on iPad

3. **Scroll down** in the share menu and find **"Add to Home Screen"**
   - You may need to scroll past other options like "Message" and "Mail"

4. **Customize the App Name** (optional)
   - Default name: "VaxTracker"
   - You can change it to whatever you prefer

5. **Tap "Add"** in the top-right corner
   - The app icon will appear on your home screen

6. **Launch the App**
   - Find the VaxTracker icon on your home screen
   - Tap it to open the app in standalone mode

---

### 💻 Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux)

#### **Chrome or Edge**

1. Open the COVID-19 Vaccine Tracker in Chrome or Edge
2. Look for the **install icon** (+) in the address bar (right side)
   - It may also appear as a computer with a down arrow
3. Click the install icon
4. Click **"Install"** in the popup dialog
5. The app will open in a standalone window
6. A shortcut will be added to your desktop and Start Menu/Applications folder

#### **Launching the Desktop App**

- Find "COVID-19 Vaccine Tracker" in your Start Menu (Windows) or Applications (macOS)
- Or double-click the desktop shortcut
- The app opens in its own window without browser tabs or address bar

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "I don't see the install option"

**Possible causes:**

- **On iOS:** You must use Safari browser (not Chrome or others)
- **On Android:** Some browsers don't support PWA (use Chrome or Edge)
- **Desktop:** The install icon may take a few seconds to appear
- **Already installed:** Check if the app is already on your home screen

**Solution:**

1. Make sure you're using a supported browser
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Clear browser cache and reload

---

### "The app doesn't work offline"

**PWA Limitations:**

- The COVID-19 Vaccine Tracker requires an internet connection for data fetching
- While the app shell is cached for faster loading, live vaccination data needs connectivity
- Some static assets (icons, CSS) will load offline, but charts and forecasts need internet

---

### "How do I uninstall the app?"

#### **Android:**

1. Long-press the app icon on your home screen
2. Tap "Remove" or "Uninstall"
3. Or go to Settings → Apps → VaxTracker → Uninstall

#### **iOS:**

1. Long-press the app icon on your home screen
2. Tap "Remove App" → "Delete App"

#### **Desktop:**

1. Open the app
2. Click the menu (⋮) → Settings → Uninstall
3. Or use your OS's standard app uninstall process
